Encryption

Without a passphrase, saved text sits in the extension's local storage in the clear, protected only by your OS user account (same as Chrome's own history and autofill). With a passphrase, every entry is encrypted with a fresh AES-256-GCM key that is wrapped to an RSA-2048 public key. Saving keeps working while locked; reading needs the passphrase, which is asked for once per browser session. There is no recovery if you forget it.
